Build a homepage for Showgate at the / route. This is the public-facing marketing page. Here are the full requirements:

Dark background, gold accents, clean and bold typography — matching the existing dashboard aesthetic. The app is called Showgate. Tagline is "The easiest way to run your event." Nigeria-first but written for a growing African audience — do not mention specific countries in the copy, keep it aspirational.

Full viewport height hero section. Large bold headline: "The easiest way to run your event." Subheadline: "Sell tickets, brand your experience, and understand your audience — all in one place." Two CTA buttons side by side: "Create Your Event" (gold, links to /signup) and "See How It Works" (outlined, scrolls to the how it works section). Two animated number counters that count up when the user scrolls to them. Three steps in a horizontal row on desktop, stacked on mobile. Each step has a number, a bold title, and two lines of description:

- Step 2: "Build your event" — Add ticket tiers, set your branding, and publish your event page in minutes.
- Step 3: "Sell and track" — Share your link, collect payments directly, and watch your analytics in real time.

Three feature cards in a grid. Bold icon, title, and short description for each:
- "Your Brand, Your Page" — Upload your logo, set your colors, and give attendees a fully branded ticket experience.

Two paragraphs of copy. We built the tools you need and got out of the way. Second paragraph: Whether you are running a concert, a conference, a church event, or a private dinner — Showgate gives you the infrastructure to sell professionally, brand boldly, and understand your audience fully.

**Section 7 — Create Your Event CTA**

Full width dark section with a centered headline: "Ready to run your next event?" Subtext: "Join organizers already using Showgate. Free to start, powerful when you need it." Single gold button: "Create Your Event" linking to /signup. If the user is already logged in, the button should say "Go to Dashboard" and link to /dashboard instead.

**Navigation**

Sticky top navbar with the Showgate logo on the left and two links on the right: "Log In" linking to /login and "Get Started" gold button linking to /signup. If user is already logged in, replace both with a "Dashboard" button.

**Footer**

Simple footer with the Showgate name, tagline, and copyright. No excessive links.

**Technical notes**

The homepage must be fully responsive for mobile. All scroll animations should be subtle — fade in on scroll, nothing distracting. The /api/stats endpoint should be public and unauthenticated. If the fetch fails, show placeholder numbers gracefully without crashing the page.
